# BeautifulBeachPark Volunteers: One-Pager

**Document:** d02-01 · **Step:** 2, Feasibility · **Version:** 1.0
· **Last updated:** 2026-09-03 · **Status:** Go
· **Owner:** Product Manager (Feasibility chat)

> **Sample document.** BeautifulBeachPark and its people are made up, and
> every cost is made up for illustration.

## 1. Problem

Coordinators find volunteers through long email chains and a paper sign-up
sheet at the park office. Slots get double-booked or left empty, and
volunteers' email addresses are shared with everyone on the chain.

## 2. Who Has It

- **Volunteers** who want to help but can't easily see which tasks still
  need people.
- **Coordinators** who spend hours each week matching people to tasks.
- **The park office**, which keeps the system running and handles problem
  volunteers.

## 3. Proposed Solution

A simple web app where coordinators post tasks such as Beach Cleanup in
one-hour slots, and volunteers sign up with just a username. Nobody sees
anyone else's email.

## 4. Why Now

The spring planting season needs about three times as many volunteers as
the winter, and the current sheet already can't keep up.

## 5. Rough Cost and Effort

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| People and time | 1 staff member guiding AI chats, about 8 weeks part-time |
| Money | Under $60 a month for hosting |
| Tools and services | Web hosting, a database, an email service |

## 6. Success Measures

| Goal | How success is measured | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Fill slots without email chains | Share of slots filled through the app | 80% within 3 months of launch |
| Save coordinators' time | Coordinator hours per week spent scheduling | Half of today's |
| Protect volunteers' privacy | Emails shown to other volunteers or coordinators | Zero |

## 7. Risks and Unknowns

- Older volunteers may not want to use an app; the paper sheet may need to
  stay for a while.
- Blocking a difficult volunteer without knowing their real name is hard.

## 8. Recommendation and Request

- **Recommendation:** Go to Requirements
- **Reason:** The problem is clear, the cost is small, and the privacy
  goal is achievable.
- **Request:** Permission to write the PRD.
